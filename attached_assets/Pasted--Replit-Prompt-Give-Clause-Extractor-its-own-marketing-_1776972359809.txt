## Replit Prompt — Give Clause Extractor its own marketing accent color

Clause Extractor still does not have a strong visible color identity on the marketing page.

### Problem
Across the marketing page, Clause Extractor currently reads too neutral/dark compared with the other 7 tools.
It needs a real accent color so it feels like a first-class tool in the same system.

## Goal
Assign Clause Extractor its own distinct accent color and apply it consistently anywhere Clause Extractor appears on the marketing site.

## Requirements

### 1. Give Clause Extractor a dedicated accent color
Pick a color family that is:
- clearly distinct from Compare Versions
- clearly distinct from Digital Signature
- clearly distinct from the neutral card background
- still consistent with the premium dark PlainPath palette

Good direction:
- a vivid violet / magenta-leaning purple
- or another premium accent that does not collide with existing tool colors

### 2. Apply Clause Extractor color consistently everywhere it appears
Audit and update all Clause Extractor appearances on the marketing page, including:
- hero pill/button
- tools/features card
- demo card / demo tab / active state
- FAQ “difference between the eight tools” answer
- any supporting chips/badges where Clause Extractor appears

For Clause Extractor, the same color family should drive:
- icon tint
- border tint
- accent underline
- chip/badge tint
- demo active-state accent

### 3. Keep contrast/readability strong
Important:
- do not make the Clause Extractor label hard to read
- keep text/background contrast strong
- do not rely on color alone; preserve icon + label + structure

### 4. Keep the rest of the system stable
This is a targeted fix, not a redesign.
Do not reshuffle other tool colors unless a direct collision is discovered.
If you do need to slightly rebalance one neighboring tool color, explain exactly why.

## Output required
1. Exact files changed
