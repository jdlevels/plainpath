B. Left-pane mirrored change indicator system
Purpose:
The left/original pane should visually show where the working-copy edits exist, without becoming editable and without showing full competing stacked edit visuals.

Behavior:
- Right pane keeps real editable ops exactly as they exist
- Left pane gets a non-destructive overlay layer
- For each active edit op in the right pane, the left pane shows a corresponding indicator at the matching page coordinates
- If multiple edits overlap, do not render messy competing indicators on the left
- Instead, group overlapping edits into one combined modified zone

Required left-pane indicator logic:
1. Non-overlapping edits
- show an indicator box at the corresponding area
- visually indicate edit type if possible

2. Overlapping edits
- compute one grouped modified zone (union of overlapping rects on same page)
- show a count badge, e.g. "2 edits", "3 edits"
- clicking or hovering the zone should identify the member edits on the right pane

3. Priority/style rules for grouped zones
If a grouped zone needs one dominant visual style, use:
- Mask / whiteout = highest priority
- Text overlay = second
- Highlight = third

4. Bidirectional hover/selection mapping
- Hover/select an edit on the right → corresponding left indicator/group highlights
- Hover/select a left indicator/group → corresponding right-side edit(s) highlight

5. Left pane remains read-only
- no editing interactions on the left pane
- no drag handles
- no text editing
- no placement on left pane
