PlainPath — App Store screenshot production brief

Goal:
Create a screenshot production plan for the locked PlainPath two-tool launch.

Do not change product code.
Do not change Analyze.
Do not change Contract Review.
Do not change billing, auth, pricing, routes, native, database, parser, or protected files.

Create:

docs/app-store-screenshot-production-plan.md

Use the existing submission doc:
docs/app-store-submission-assets.md

Locked launch tools:
1. Analyze a Document — complete paperwork before submission
2. Contract Review — understand contract terms before signing

Plan a 6-screenshot iPhone set.

Use iPhone 6.9-inch portrait as the primary screenshot target.
Use Apple-accepted screenshot dimensions for 6.9-inch iPhone portrait.
Include notes if additional 6.5-inch screenshots are required or if App Store Connect can scale from the highest-resolution required screenshots.

For each screenshot, include:
1. Screenshot title
2. Overlay caption
3. Exact app screen to capture
4. Required state/data
5. What it proves
6. What not to show

Screenshot set:

1. Start / Upload
Caption idea:
“Upload paperwork or a contract.”

2. Analyze — Understand
Caption idea:
“See what the document says in plain English.”

3. Analyze — Requirements
Caption idea:
“Know what needs to be completed.”

4. Analyze — Complete
Caption idea:
“Check off items as you finish them.”

5. Contract Review — Key Clauses
Caption idea:
“Review key clauses before signing.”

6. Contract Review — Questions to Ask
Caption idea:
“Know what to clarify before you sign.”

Rules:
- Use fictional/demo documents only
- No real personal data
- No attorney-approved claims
- No guaranteed-safe claims
- No do-not-sign language
- No removed tools
- No Starter / Team / annual pricing
- No print/export-focused screenshot as a primary screenshot
- Captions must be short and readable on mobile
