Required improvements:

The PDF viewer has a large amount of unused black margin while the right analysis panel feels squeezed.

Update the desktop layout so the analysis side gets more usable width.

If the split is configurable, default closer to 50/50.

Apply a light premium readability pass to all result tabs:
- Plain English
- Source Sections
- Overview
- What’s Missing

- Risks & Notes

The goal is cleaner and easier to scan.

Issue:
Some panels, especially Source Sections breakdown, show internal scrollbars that make the UI feel less premium.

Preferred:

Do not let every important card become red.
Deadline and risk pages should feel serious, not visually overwhelming.
