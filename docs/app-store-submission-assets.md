## 2. App Description

*(App Store limit: 4,000 characters. This draft is approximately 900 characters — intentionally lean and scannable.)*

---

**Short version (recommended):**

PlainPath helps you understand confusing paperwork before you act on it.

Whether you're dealing with a form, a lease, a medical bill, or a contract someone else wrote — PlainPath reads it and tells you what it means, what you need, and what to ask.

**Two tools included:**

**Analyze a Document**
Upload or paste any document — a lease, government notice, medical bill, permit application, or legal letter. PlainPath extracts what it means in plain English: required documents, deadlines, signatures needed, and what to do next. Know what to complete before you submit.

**Contract Review**
Before you sign, PlainPath reads the fine print. It identifies clauses that need attention, flags what's missing, gives you the balance of terms, and prepares questions you can ask the other party. Know what you're agreeing to before you sign.

**What PlainPath is:**
A plain-English document tool that helps you understand paperwork, organize your response, and know the right questions to ask.

**What PlainPath is not:**
A legal advice service. PlainPath does not tell you whether to sign or submit. For legal decisions, consult a qualified attorney.

PlainPath Pro — $19.99/month. Both tools included. Cancel anytime.

## 7. App Review Notes

*(Paste into the "Notes for App Review" field in App Store Connect.)*

PlainPath is a document understanding tool that helps users read and interpret confusing paperwork before taking action.

**What the app does:**
- Analyze a Document: Users upload or paste a document. The app returns a plain-English summary, required documents, deadlines, risks, and action steps.
