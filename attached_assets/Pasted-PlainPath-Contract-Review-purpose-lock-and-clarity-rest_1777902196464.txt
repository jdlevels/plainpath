PlainPath — Contract Review purpose lock and clarity restructure

Goal:
Restructure Contract Review around its correct purpose:
Help users quickly understand what a contract says, what clauses matter, what obligations they are accepting, what risks exist, and what they should clarify before signing.

This should feel like a clean contract parser and before-you-sign guide — not a legal report, not a generic document analysis, and not a cluttered audit screen.

Do not touch:
- Analyze a Document
- billing
- RevenueCat
- Stripe
- native billing
- auth
- pricing
- homepage
- marketing
- routes
- database schema
- removed tools

Do not rebuild broadly.
Do not add new product surfaces.
Do not provide legal advice.
Do not say whether the user should or should not sign.
Use “review,” “clarify,” and “confirm” language.

Core Contract Review purpose:
Contract Review helps users quickly understand:
1. what the contract is
2. what it says
3. what clauses matter
4. what obligations the user is accepting
5. what risks may exist
6. what is missing or unclear
7. what to clarify before signing

Primary user question:
“What am I agreeing to, what should I watch for, and what should I clarify before I sign?”

Recommended user-facing structure:
Summary → Key Clauses → Risks → Before You Sign

1. Summary section

Make this short and scannable.

Show:
- contract type
- parties
- purpose
- term
- payment / money terms
- main obligations
- top issue to review first

Avoid long paragraphs.
Avoid repeating all clauses here.

2. Key Clauses section

This is the main parser output.

Display important clauses as clean cards:
- Payment
- Term / renewal
- Termination
- Confidentiality
- Liability limit
- Warranty / disclaimer
- Intellectual property
- Indemnity
- Governing law
- Dispute resolution
- Notices
- Assignment
- Acceptance / approval terms
- Signature / execution terms

Each clause card should show:
- Clause name
- What it says
- Why it matters
- What to check before signing
- Source quote or clause reference
- Risk level

Keep source quotes visually secondary or collapsible if long.

3. Risks section

Only show actual risks, not every clause.

Each risk should show:
- Risk title
- Why it matters
- Possible consequence
- What to clarify
- Source quote

Avoid red overload.
Use severity carefully:
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Needs clarification

No legal conclusions.
No “do not sign” language.
Use “consider reviewing with a qualified professional if needed.”

4. Before You Sign section

Create a simple checklist-style section.

Show items like:
- Confirm payment amount and due dates
- Confirm renewal/cancellation terms
- Clarify vague deliverables or acceptance terms
- Confirm who must sign
- Ask for missing exhibit/schedule/attachment
- Confirm liability limits
- Save a signed copy

If persistence/checkoff is not already built for Contract Review, keep this read-only for now.
Do not add localStorage or new completion state in this pass.

5. Clutter reduction

Remove or consolidate sections that duplicate each other.

Rules:
- Summary explains the contract.
- Key Clauses explains clauses.
- Risks explains what may hurt the user.
- Before You Sign explains what to clarify or confirm.

Do not show the same item in three places unless each section adds a distinct purpose.

6. Mobile and desktop readability

Mobile:
- clause cards must be readable
- no horizontal overflow
- important clause/risk labels must be tappable/readable
- no tiny badge-only meaning

Desktop:
- maintain professional split view
- avoid dense wall-of-text cards
- keep right panel scannable

7. Trust and safety language

Keep:
- source-backed quotes
- manual-review language when uncertain
- no legal advice disclaimer
- no guarantee that contract is safe
- no instruction that user should or should not sign

Use:
- “Review”
- “Clarify”
- “Confirm”
- “Ask before signing”
- “May need attention”

Avoid:
- “This is illegal”
- “You should sign”
- “Do not sign”
- “Guaranteed safe”
- “Attorney-approved”

8. QA

Verify:
- Contract Review opens
- Contract Review demo opens
- Summary is short and clear
- Key Clauses are easy to scan
- Risks are not duplicative
- Before You Sign is actionable
- Source quotes still appear
- Mobile layout is readable
- Desktop layout remains premium
- No Analyze changes
- No billing/auth/native/Stripe/RevenueCat changes
- No removed tool references
- Build passes
