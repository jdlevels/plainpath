2. Replace continuous scroll sync with linked navigation
Do not hard-lock raw continuous scroll mirroring between panes in V1.

3. Use AI only on changed chunks later
Do not run semantic AI blindly across the whole document pair in V1.

- deterministic extraction + diff first
