## Long Description (shared base — adapt per store)

PlainPath turns complex documents into plain English — instantly, privately, and without a lawyer.

Whether you're signing a lease, reviewing a job offer, navigating a medical form, or protecting your information before sharing, PlainPath gives you a clear, honest breakdown of what a document actually says — and what it means for you.

**5 tools. One app.**

**Analyze** — Paste or upload any document and get a full plain-English breakdown: what it says, key terms defined, important deadlines, required actions, and risks you should know about.

**Trust Check** — Get an honest second opinion on any document. PlainPath flags language designed to limit your rights, confusing clauses, and patterns worth questioning before you sign.

**Contract Builder** — Answer a few questions and PlainPath drafts a complete, clear contract — NDAs, service agreements, freelance contracts, and more — in language both sides can understand.

**Contract Review** — Upload a contract you received. PlainPath checks it clause by clause: what's standard, what's unusual, what's missing, and what you may want to push back on.

**Redact Sensitive Info** — Before sharing any document, scan it for personal information (names, SSNs, account numbers, emails, phone numbers, and more), review each item, and permanently remove what you don't want to share. Exported output is a clean redacted text version.

---

**Who it's for:**
- Renters reviewing leases
- Freelancers reading contracts
- Job seekers evaluating offer letters
- Caregivers navigating healthcare paperwork
- Anyone who receives legal documents and wants to actually understand them

**Privacy-first by design:**
Your documents are processed to generate analysis and then discarded. PlainPath does not store your document content long-term. Redacted output is generated entirely on your device output side — original files are not modified.
