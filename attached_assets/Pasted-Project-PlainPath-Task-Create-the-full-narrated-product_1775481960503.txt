-----------------------------------

The video should explain the product as one system with three lanes:

2. Document Trust Check
- review suspicious documents before paying, signing, or responding
- show verdict + score model + verification guidance

3. Build a Contract
- guide the user through structured intake
- surface missing protections
- generate a draft-ready result

The video should make these feel connected, not like three random products.

Build the video around this story arc:
