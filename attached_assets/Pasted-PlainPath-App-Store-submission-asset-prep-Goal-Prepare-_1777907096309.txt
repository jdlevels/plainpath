PlainPath — App Store submission asset prep

Goal:
Prepare App Store submission assets for the locked two-tool PlainPath launch.

Do not change product code.
Do not rebuild tools.
Do not change Analyze a Document.
Do not change Contract Review.
Do not change billing, auth, pricing, routes, native, database, parser, or protected files.

Locked launch tools:
1. Analyze a Document
   Purpose: Complete paperwork before submission.
2. Contract Review
   Purpose: Understand contract terms before signing.

Create a submission prep document:

docs/app-store-submission-assets.md

Include:

1. App identity

App name:
PlainPath

Recommended subtitle options:
- Understand paperwork faster
- Documents made clearer
- Review documents before acting

Select the strongest option and explain why.

2. App description

Write a clear App Store-ready description.

Core positioning:
PlainPath helps people understand confusing paperwork, identify what needs attention, and know what to complete or ask before submitting or signing.

Include the two launch tools:
- Analyze a Document
- Contract Review

Do not mention removed tools.
Do not mention unreleased tools.
Do not overclaim legal advice.
Do not say attorney-approved.
Do not say guaranteed safe.
Do not say the app replaces a lawyer.

3. Promotional text

Write a short promotional text under 170 characters.

Suggested direction:
“Upload paperwork or a contract and get clear answers, required items, risks, and questions to ask before you submit or sign.”

4. Keywords

Prepare keyword candidates focused on:
document review, contract review, paperwork, forms, plain English, document scanner, document checklist, lease, agreement, legal document, PDF review

Keep within Apple keyword-style constraints: comma-separated, no phrases that overclaim legal services.

5. Screenshot plan

Create a 6-screenshot set for iPhone.

Use screenshot dimensions from Apple’s current requirements. Prioritize 6.9-inch iPhone portrait screenshots.

Screenshots should show:
1. Upload or start screen
2. Analyze — Understand mode
3. Analyze — Requirements / completion checklist
4. Contract Review — Summary
5. Contract Review — Key Clauses with Questions to Ask Before Signing
6. Before You Sign / final clarity screen

For each screenshot, include:
- screenshot title
- short overlay caption
- screen to capture
- what it proves

Avoid:
- fake legal claims
- private user data
- “guaranteed safe”
- “attorney-approved”
- “do not sign”
- fear-based wording

6. Privacy summary

Draft a plain-English privacy summary aligned with the current app policy:
- documents are processed to return results
- documents are not sold
- documents are not used to train AI models
- user account / email may be used for account and subscription access
- third-party services may process data needed for app functionality

Do not invent technical details.
Flag anything that requires owner confirmation.

7. Reviewer notes

Draft App Review notes explaining:
- PlainPath is a document understanding tool
- It provides plain-English summaries, required items, clause explanations, and questions to ask
- It does not provide legal advice
- It does not tell users whether to sign or submit documents
- Stripe/external payment is hidden in native iOS
- Subscription access uses native entitlement flow / RevenueCat if configured
- Demo content is fictional

8. URL checklist

Confirm or list required URLs:
- Support URL
- Privacy Policy URL
- Terms of Service URL
- Marketing URL

9. Final output

Return:
- file created
- recommended app subtitle
- final app description
- screenshot list
- privacy/reviewer notes summary
- missing owner-confirmation items
- final recommendation
