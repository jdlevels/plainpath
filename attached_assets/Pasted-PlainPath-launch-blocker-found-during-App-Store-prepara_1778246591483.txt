PlainPath launch blocker found during App Store preparation.

Issue:
On the live website, clicking Sign Up redirects to:

accounts.plainpathapp.com/sign-up

Chrome shows:

ERR_SSL_VERSION_OR_CIPHER_MISMATCH

This blocks account creation, Apple reviewer login setup, and App Store submission.

Task:
Run a production launch-blocker audit focused on auth, paywalls, reviewer access, and iOS submission readiness.

Do not build new features.
Do not unlock hidden tools.
Do not redesign UI.
Do not change pricing.
Do not change schema.

Part 1 — Auth domain diagnosis
Confirm:
- What auth provider is being used.
- Whether accounts.plainpathapp.com is intended to be a Clerk/custom auth domain.
- Whether accounts.plainpathapp.com has the correct DNS record.
- Whether the SSL certificate is valid and active.
- Whether the app redirects to the correct production sign-up/sign-in URL.
- Whether the issue is DNS, SSL, Clerk custom domain verification, or app config.

Acceptance:
- Sign Up works from plainpathapp.com.
- Sign In works from plainpathapp.com.
- No SSL/cipher mismatch appears.
- Auth works in incognito/private browser.
