PART 2 — CONTRACT BUILDER “CHECK FOR GAPS & FILL”
-----------------------------------

Inside the Contract Builder intake/review flow, add a structured review layer that checks for missing protections, incomplete business terms, and common contract omissions before draft generation.

Best placement:
- Review Summary step
- clearly visible section titled:
  “Check for Gaps & Fill Recommendations”

This section should scan the intake answers and surface items such as:
- no late fee
- no kill fee
- no client feedback deadline
- no revision cap
- no delivery deadline
- no payment due window
- no governing law
- no termination rule
- ownership transfers before final payment
- no dispute resolution method
- no notice method

Behavior:
- show plain-English warnings/recommendations
- distinguish between:
  - recommended business protections
  - default legal protections already added

Examples:
- ⚠ No late fee included
- ⚠ No kill fee included
- ⚠ No client-delay rule included
- ✅ Default severability clause added
- ✅ Default force majeure clause added
- ✅ Default notices clause added

For each recommended gap, allow the user to:
- keep as-is
- add recommended protection
- answer a quick follow-up question
- use standard default if appropriate

Important rule:
- Do NOT silently add major business terms
- It is okay to auto-add neutral legal scaffolding
- Business-impacting terms must be surfaced as recommendations or require confirmation
