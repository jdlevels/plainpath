# PlainPath — Homepage Demo Video Script & Shot List

**Target runtime:** 75 seconds (acceptable: 60–90 s)  
**Voice style:** calm · professional · plain English · steady pacing  
**Tone:** modern SaaS — confident, clear, not overhyped

---

## 1. Demo Concept Summary

**What it communicates:** PlainPath turns confusing paperwork into clear action — across three connected tools: Analyze, Trust Check, and Build.  
**Intended audience:** Anyone who receives legal or official documents they can't fully interpret.  
**Desired tone:** Premium, trustworthy, direct. No gimmicks.

## 6. Editing Guidance

- **Transitions:** cross-dissolve between scenes (0.2–0.3 s). No hard cuts except on action moments (e.g., click to load).
- **Pacing:** match voiceover — no idle time. Each scene cuts when the VO moves on.
- **Captions:** add the on-screen text from the shot list as lower-thirds or centered overlays (white text on semi-transparent dark pill, same style as the PlainPath UI).
- **UI zooms:** use subtle slow zooms (3–5% over 2–3 s) to draw attention to key elements. Avoid jarring zooms.
- **Blur/focus:** optionally blur surrounding UI when highlighting a specific card/badge. Useful for the deadline card and trust score.
- **Click highlights:** add a subtle circular ripple on every real click action so viewers can track cursor movement.
- **Section cards:** add a brief full-frame section title card (0.5 s, fade in/out) between the three tool sections. E.g. — "1 · Analyze", "2 · Verify", "3 · Build".

---

## 7. Audio Guidance

- **Music:** light, minimal background track (ambient / lo-fi / soft piano). Keep it at -18 dB relative to voice. No melody that competes with narration.
- **Pacing:** one breath pause after Scene 3 (after "what to do first"), one after Scene 5 (after "so you're not guessing"), one before Scene 10 closing.
- **Voice recording:** record in a quiet room with a condenser mic or quality USB mic. No compression artifacts. Single take preferred for natural flow.
- **Music fade:** fade music out over the final 3 seconds. Let the closing tagline land in near-silence.

## Activating the Video on the Homepage

Once recorded and uploaded (YouTube or CDN), open:

```
artifacts/plainpath/src/components/VideoWalkthrough.tsx
```

And set `DEMO_VIDEO_URL` on line 12 to either:

- **YouTube embed:** `"https://www.youtube.com/embed/YOUR_VIDEO_ID"`
- **Direct MP4:** `"https://cdn.example.com/plainpath-demo.mp4"`

The player auto-detects the format and renders the correct embed.
