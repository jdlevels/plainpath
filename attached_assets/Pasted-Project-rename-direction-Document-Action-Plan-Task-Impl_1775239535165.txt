3. Trust-check result schema
Create a dedicated result schema with these sections:

A. Risk Verdict
- one of:
  - Likely legitimate
  - Suspicious — verify before acting
  - High scam risk
  - Cannot verify authenticity
- include a short explanation of why

B. What This Letter Is Claiming
- plain explanation of what the document says it is about

C. Demanded Action
- what the document is asking the user to do

D. Scam Indicators
- suspicious signals found in the content
- pressure tactics
- payment red flags
- sender inconsistencies
- risky fee/escalation language
- sensitive information requests
- impersonation-style wording if present

E. Suspicious Contact Details
- phone numbers found
- email addresses found
- URLs/domains found
- mailing/return addresses if present
- flag what needs verification

F. Deadlines / Pressure Tactics
- explicit dates
- relative urgency phrases
- shutdown / arrest / legal / repossession / collection / service interruption pressure language
- fee hike / APR increase / late fee / cutoff windows if present

G. What To Verify
- what the user should independently verify before acting

H. Safe Next Steps
- concrete safe actions such as:
  - do not call the number in the letter yet
  - verify through the official website directly
  - do not click payment links
  - do not send money until independently verified
  - preserve the document for reporting

5. Risk scoring
Implement a first-pass weighted risk model.

Suggested severity classes:

High severity:
- gift card / crypto / wire transfer demand
- threat of arrest
- suspicious payment link
- request for SSN / banking / card details in a risky context
- sender/payment mismatch
- immediate shutoff threat with suspicious contact path

Medium severity:
- short deadline pressure
- aggressive legal language without specific case/reference support
- unusual fee escalation
- mismatched branding/contact details
- missing case/account/reference identifiers

Low severity:
- grammar/spelling issues
- generic greeting
- vague sender identity
- formatting inconsistencies

Map score to verdict:
- 0–24: Likely legitimate
- 25–49: Cannot verify authenticity
- 50–74: Suspicious — verify before acting
- 75–100: High scam risk

If the existing code structure needs slightly different thresholds, keep the same 4 verdict outputs.

6. Trust-check prompt behavior
The trust-check mode must use risk-based wording only.

Do NOT output:
- definitely fake
- proven fraud
- legally invalid

Allowed framing:
- shows scam indicators
- appears suspicious
- cannot be independently verified from the provided content
- should be verified through official channels before acting
