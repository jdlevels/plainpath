====================================================

- Tool name
- Short verb or action phrase
- Plain-English benefit
- 3–4 feature notes
- Correct CTA
- Correct route
- Correct color/icon style
- No “coming soon” unless truly not available

Required cards:

1. Analyze a Document
Purpose: Understand any document in plain English with actions, deadlines, missing items, and risks.

2. Document Trust Check
Purpose: Check whether a document looks suspicious, inconsistent, high-risk, or unverifiable before acting.

3. Build a Contract
Purpose: Answer guided questions and generate a structured contract draft.

4. Contract Review
Purpose: Review contract clauses, red flags, watch-outs, missing protections, and negotiation notes.

5. Redact Sensitive Info
Purpose: Find and permanently cover sensitive information before sharing.

6. Ask This Document
Purpose: Ask questions about a document and receive plain-English answers tied to the file.

7. Clause Extractor
Purpose: Extract key clauses, dates, obligations, parties, and missing items from contracts or agreements.

8. Compare Versions
Purpose: Compare two versions of a document and identify additions, deletions, changed terms, and risk shifts.

9. Document Builder
Purpose: Create SOPs, manuals, policies, checklists, onboarding guides, and internal business documents.
