4. REDUCE “RED WALL” EFFECT
--------------------------------------------------

Current problem:
Red appears too heavily across some tabs, which makes the page feel alarming and less refined.

Enforce severity color rules:

Critical / High:
- red
- only for serious consequence, missed deadline, money loss, rights loss, denial, termination, fraud, or major contractual issue

Medium:
- amber/orange
- review carefully, possible cost/risk, needs confirmation

Low / Reference:
- blue/neutral
- context, general timing, informational notes

Confirmed:
- green

Missing / Needs Input:
- blue or purple
- only red if it blocks a major action or deadline

Refine red cards:
- use softer red backgrounds
- keep red border/label visible
- reduce full-card red saturation
- make red feel serious but not visually overwhelming

Acceptance:
High-risk sections should look urgent, but not like an error screen.
