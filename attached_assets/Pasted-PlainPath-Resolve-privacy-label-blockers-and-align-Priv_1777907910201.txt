2. Privacy Policy alignment

If the Privacy Policy currently implies that no document content is retained, update it to be accurate.

Use clear language like:

“PlainPath does not sell your documents or use them to train AI models. Uploaded files are processed to provide your results. PlainPath may store extracted document text, document metadata, and generated analysis results when needed to provide saved analysis history, account access, and app functionality.”

Also include:

“You can contact support to request deletion of account-related analysis data, subject to legal, security, and operational retention requirements.”

Do not overclaim.
Do not say original uploaded files are deleted unless confirmed.
Do not say no document content is stored if extracted text/results are stored.
